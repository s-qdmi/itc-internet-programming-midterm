About eLibrary

eLibrary's goal is to combine the advantages of physical and digital literature. By providing digital and audio copies of a book, users are not fully constrained by the "time limit" of traditional borrowing but can still enjoy a screenless reading experience. eLibrary's loose DRM for digital copies allows readers to permanently own a copy what they've read for an affordable price.
